## 1. Summary

Vector Scope AI LLC proposes two connected historical walking-tour maps that help residents and visitors explore Afton's Old Village and Valley Creek through historical maps, photographs, narrated stories, and selected 3D reconstructions. The project includes 20 tour stops, an accessible HTML presentation of every stop independent of the 3D scene, and a maintainable website entered from a Washington County landing map. Work is planned for October 2026 through September 2027, contingent on grant funding and guided by the Heritage Preservation Commission (HPC) and the History Consultant.

**Fixed-price total: $19,400**

## 2. Working prototype (included at no charge)

A working prototype, "Afton Historic Atlas", is already built and can be demonstrated to the HPC at [jwitcoski.github.io/AftonHistoric](https://jwitcoski.github.io/AftonHistoric/). What is on that site today:

- A Washington County landing map with the county's official GIS layers (parcels, streets, parks, plats, and more). The county history introduction is placeholder copy adapted from Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0) and will be replaced with reviewed local sources.
- A guided Old Village path: an introduction, ten stop cards, and the era changing at each stop. The ten stops are drawn from public HistoricPlace and City-designated historic site pages. Locations, wording, and selection will be corrected with the HPC and the History Consultant.
- Era switching among 1855 (founding), 1971 (incorporation), and today. The 1855 and 1971 views are illustrative: they change which present-day layers are visible. They are not verified reconstructions.
- Orbit and zoom on laptop and mobile, and a layer control to show or hide map layers.
- In the 3D scene: terrain, plus OpenStreetMap roads, buildings, and water. County GIS is on the landing map. Stop records are ready for photographs and audio. The first stop includes one labeled sample photograph and one short synthesized narration with a transcript. The other nine stops stay text-only. Each stop shows the present-day Washington County parcel pin that contains it.

Photographs, narration, plat overlays, period building models, and an accessible HTML tour of every stop are production work. In the prototype, stop text appears in the map popup.

**How to view it**

1. Open [jwitcoski.github.io/AftonHistoric](https://jwitcoski.github.io/AftonHistoric/).
2. On the county map, open COUNTY GIS LAYERS and turn on a layer such as parcels.
3. Choose Afton's Old Village Tour.
4. Choose "Take Afton's Old Village tour," then use Next stop to move through the ten sites.
5. Use the 1855, 1971, and Today buttons to switch eras. Drag to rotate and scroll to zoom.
6. Valley Creek is shown as coming soon. It is not built in the prototype.

The 3D scene uses simple extruded building footprints. Present-day GIS and OpenStreetMap features are context for today and will not be presented as historical geometry. Production historical claims, site locations, and reconstructed features will be supported by cited sources and reviewed with the HPC and History Consultant. Unsourced visual details will be labeled interpretive. Deliverable I replaces selected extruded footprints with sourced exterior models. Project funds go to researched content, those models, design, accessibility, and testing.
