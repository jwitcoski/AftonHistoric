## 1. Summary

We propose to design and build two multi-layered, audio-enabled interactive historical maps for the City of Afton: the **Old Village Tour** (10 sites) and the **Valley Creek Tour** (10 sites). We will also photograph at least 10 Valley Creek historic sites and prepare all photos and historic documents for the online tour site. Work runs October 2026 through September 2027, contingent on grant funding, and is guided by the Heritage Preservation Commission (HPC).

**Fixed-price total: $13,000**

## 2. Working prototype (included at no charge)

A working prototype, "Afton Historic Atlas", is already built and can be demonstrated to the HPC. It is a browser-based 3D map with:

- Era switching (1855 founding, 1971 incorporation, today)
- Orbit/zoom navigation on laptop and mobile
- A layer control to show or hide map layers
- Terrain and OpenStreetMap-derived overlays, shown only for the present day
- A site-marker system with photo and audio fields
- Source attribution (MapTiler, OpenStreetMap)

The prototype contains no fabricated historical data. Historic claims, plats and sites will be added only from sources reviewed with the HPC and the History Consultant. The production maps extend this codebase, so the City's budget goes to content, design and accessibility rather than to building a viewer from scratch. This is why the price is well below a custom build.
